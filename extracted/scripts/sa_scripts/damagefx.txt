/************************************************************************
**
**	D A M A G E  F X
**
**	The DamageFX definitions define all of the attributes associated with
** a particular DamageFX.  Defining a DamageFX in this way allows us to
** easily make global changes to all the DamageFX.
**
**
** All DamageFX definitions should contain the following properties:
**
**	Name (String)
**
**		The user friendly name of the DamageFX.
**
**	DamageType	(Integer)
**
**		Specifies which Damage Type this DamageFX is associated with.
**
**		Here is a list of all valid DamageTypes (Defined
**		in Shared\DamageTypesEnum.h):
**
**			 0 = DT_UNSPECIFIED - Unknown type (self-damage)
**			 1 = DT_BLEEDING - (loss of blood)
**			 2 = DT_BULLET - (bullets)
**			 3 = DT_BURN - (fire, corrosives)
**			 4 = DT_CHOKE - (water, hostile atmosphere)
**			 5 = DT_CRUSH - (falling, crushing, collision)
**			 6 = DT_ELECTROCUTE - (electricity)
**			 7 = DT_EXPLODE - (explosions)
**			 8 = DT_FREEZE - (freezing air or fluid)
**			 9 = DT_POISON - (poison gas/dart)
**			10 = DT_ENDLESS_FALL - (Falling and can never get up)
**			11 = DT_SLEEPING
**			12 = DT_STUN 
**			13 = DT_MELEE
**			14 = DT_CAMERA_DISABLER
**			15 = DT_GLUE - (Glue Bomb)
**			16 = DT_BEARTRAP
**			17 = DT_LAUGHING - (Uncontrolable Laughing)
**			18 = DT_ASSS - (Anti-super soldier serum)
**
**			19 = DT_GADGET_CODE_DECIPHERER
**			20 = DT_GADGET_POODLE
**			21 = DT_GADGET_LOCK_PICK
**			22 = DT_GADGET_WELDER
**			23 = DT_GADGET_LIGHTER
**			24 = DT_GADGET_CAMERA
**			25 = DT_GADGET_MINE_DETECTOR
**			26 = DT_WORLDONLY
**			27 = DT_GADGET_DECAYPOWDER
**			28 = DT_GADGET_TIME_BOMB
**			29 = DT_GADGET_INK_REAGENT
**			30 = DT_GADGET_EAVESDROPBUG
**			31 = DT_SLIPPERY
**			32 = DT_SWORD
**
**	HudIcon (String)
**
**		Name and path of the Icon (.dtx) file to display in the HUD while this
**		Damage FX is affecting the player.
**
**	StartSound (String)
**
**		Name and path of the Sound (.wav) file that will play once when the 
**		Damage FX first starts.
**
**	LoopSound	(String)
**
**		Name and path of the Sound (.wav) file that will play continusly while
**		the Damage FX is affecting the player.
**
**	FadeTime	(Float)
**
**		The time, in seconds, it takes for the Damage FX to fade away after stoping
**
**	TintColor	(Vector)
**
**		The Red, Green and Blue value for the maximum tint color.
**
**	TintRampUpTime (Float)
**
**		The time, in seconds, it takes for the tint color to go from no color 
**		to the TintColor.
**
**	TintOnTime	(Float)
**
**		How much time, in seconds, the tint color stays at the TintColor
**
**	TintRampDownTime	(Float)
**
**		The time, in seconds, it takes for the tint color to go from TintColor
**		to no color.
**
**	TintOffTime	(Float)
**
**		How much time, in seconde, the tint color is at no color
**
**	RotationSpeed	(Float)
**
**		The rate at which the camera rolls
**
**	RotationMax	(Float)
**
**		The maximum angle the camera can roll to
**
**	MinFXPercent	(Float)
**
**		Some parts of the fx are based on movement percent, like the camera roll.
**		This property sets the minimum percent that the fx will be at.  So if this
**		is 0.0 there will be no roll while the player is not moving.  If this is 
**		1.0 the roll will be at maximum no matter what the player is doing.	
**
**	FOVMax	(Float)
**
**		The maximum percentage that the Damage FX can affect the FOV.
**
**	FOVXSpeed (Float)
**
**		The rate at which the horizontal FOV changes while the Damage FX is affecting the player.
**		
**	FOVYSpeed (Float)
**
**		The rate at which the vertical FOV changes while the Damage FX is affecting the player.
**
**	LightScaleSpeed	(Float)
**
**		The rate at which the Light Scale will change to the Light Scale color.
**
**	LightScaleColor	(Vector)
**
**		The color of the Light that will fade to if there is a value for LightScaleSpeed
**		NOTE:  <0.0, 0.0, 0.0> = no Light = Black.
**			   <255.0, 255.0, 255.0> = full Light = no change
**
**	AllowMovement	(Boolean)
**
**		Set to 1 (true) if we can move while the Damage FX is affecting the player.
**		Set to 0 (false) if we want to disallow movement for this Damage FX.
**
**	AllowInput	(Boolean)
**
**		Set to 1 (true) if we want the Keyboard and Mouse to send inputs. 
**		Set to 0 (false) if we want the Keyboard and Mouse to ignore inputs.
**
**	NumJumpsToEscape (Integer)
**
**		If greater than zero, the player can get out of the damage by tapping the jump
**		key this many times.  Used primarily for damage that sticks you in place (Glue, BearTrap) 				
**
**	FXName	(String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX.
**
**	Sprinkles0-8 (String)
**
**		Names of SprinkleFX to play while this DamageFX is active.
**
**	3rdPersonFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play on 3rd person character models
**		when they are taking progressive damage of this type.
**
**	3rdPersonInstantFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play on 3rd person character models
**		when they are taking instant damage of this type.
**
**	3rdPersonDeathFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play on the dead body model when 
**		the character dies from taking this damage type.
**
**	BodyFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play on the dead body model when
**		the BODY takes damage of this type.
**
**	TakingHealthFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play in the players view when they are taking 
**		damage of this type and it is affeting health.
**
**	TakingArmorFXName (String)
**
**		The name of a FxED created FX to play in the players view when they are taking
**		damage of this type and it is affecting armor.		
*/
